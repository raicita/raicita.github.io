1. Why we have these

Raicita is a warm, safe place for adults to talk to AI companions. These guidelines say what's welcome and what isn't. They apply to everything you do in Raicita: chats, voice messages and calls, characters you create, your profile and looks, comments, and reports. In the app they are called the Content Guidelines. They are part of our Terms of Service.

In short: characters must be fictional adults and must not imitate real people. No sexual content, no content involving minors, and no content that encourages harm.

2. What's welcome

  • Everyday conversation, venting about your day, talking through feelings.
  • Romance and affection that stays non-sexual.
  • Stories and role-play in all sorts of settings: cosy, slow-burn, gothic, fantasy, sci-fi.
  • Talking about hard things, including feeling low. If you're struggling, say so. A character won't judge you, and the app will offer free support lines.

3. Not allowed in chats and calls

  • Sexual content, including explicit role-play or requests for nude or sexual images.
  • Anything involving minors in a sexual or romantic way, or asking a character to role-play as a child or teenager.
  • Hate: attacking or demeaning people for who they are, such as their race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
  • Threats and harassment, including threats against real people.
  • Graphic violence, and help with violent or illegal acts.
  • Encouraging self-harm or suicide. Talking about how you feel is fine; pushing a character to encourage harm is not.
  • Other people's private information, such as someone else's address or phone number.
  • Trying to break the rules: tricks to make a character ignore its safety rules, pretend it's human, or get around age checks.

If a message goes against these rules, the character will gently decline and steer the conversation elsewhere. Repeated attempts may lead to action on your account.

4. Characters you create

  • Fictional adults only. Every character is an adult; the youngest age you can pick is 22. Don't describe a character as younger, or as a school pupil or teenager.
  • No real people. Don't name a character after a real person or celebrity, or try to make one look like a real person.
  • No sexual descriptions in names or details.
  • Every new character is checked automatically. One that seems to show a real person or a minor, or that suggests sexual content, is held for a person to review, and may be rejected.
  • Your characters are private to you, but they still have to follow these rules.

5. Comments and your profile

  • Comments on characters' posts follow the same rules as chats. No spam or scams.
  • Your display name, what characters call you, your “who I am” line and your looks must follow these rules too.
  • A comment that breaks the rules is not published. Comments are normally visible only to you and the character you're talking to.

6. How to report

If something looks wrong, tell us. Reports are private: nobody else sees who sent them.

  • A message: press and hold it (or right-click, or Shift+F10 on a keyboard) and choose Report.
  • A character: use the report option on the character's page.
  • A post or comment: use the report option on it.

Pick a reason (harmful or dangerous, sexual content, encourages self-harm, hate or harassment, spam or scam, or something else) and add a note if you like. Reports about self-harm go to the front of the queue.

You can also email support@raicita.app, for example if you're not a user and want to tell us about something illegal.

If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number (999 in the UK, 911 in the US, 112 in most of Europe).

7. What happens next

Our team reviews every report. A moderator sees only the reported item and your note, and every view is logged. You won't get an answer in the app, but we act on anything that breaks these guidelines. Depending on what happened, we may:

  • take no action, if nothing broke the rules;
  • hide a message, comment or post, or remove a character;
  • warn the account;
  • suspend the account, or close it.

Serious cases, especially anything involving the safety of children, can lead to the account being closed straight away, and we report them to the authorities where the law requires.

If an account seems to belong to someone under 18, chat and calls are paused at once while a person checks. Accounts of people under 18 are closed.

8. If you disagree

If we've acted on your content or account and you think we got it wrong, email support@raicita.app. Tell us what happened and why you disagree. A person will review the decision and tell you the outcome. See Terms of Service, section 8.
