How we check content

We use automated checks on messages, voice transcripts, comments, characters and generated images. Some checks also look for signs that you may be in crisis or that a user may be under 18. More on this in AI & Safety and the Privacy Policy.

Reporting

You can report a character, a message, a post or a comment in the app (tap and hold, or use the menu). You can also email support@raicita.app, including if you are not a user and want to tell us about illegal content. Reports about self-harm are reviewed first.

What we may do

After reviewing a report or a flag we may take no action, hide the content, remove a character, warn you, suspend your account or close it. When we take action against your content or account, we will tell you, and explain why, unless the law or safety reasons prevent it.

Appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong, email support@raicita.app and tell us which decision you mean and why. A person will look at it again and tell you the outcome.   • If we change the price of your subscription, we will tell you before the new price applies, and you can cancel before then.
  • Subscriptions can only be paid by card (including Apple Pay and Google Pay where available).
