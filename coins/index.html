1. What coins are for

Everything a character does for you in Raicita is paid for with coins: replying to a message, a voice message, a photo or short clip, each minute of a call, a Moment together, a gift, a portrait for a character you create, and a character's reply to your comment.

  • Everyone pays the same prices. There are no free allowances or unlimited plans.
  • The app shows what each thing costs on the Coins screen, and before you spend.
  • A text message is only charged when the AI actually replies. If the reply fails or is replaced by a safety message, the coins come back.
  • Safety replies are always free, even with no coins left.
  • If a photo, clip or portrait can't be made, its coins come back automatically.
  • Calls are charged at the start of each minute. You'll get a warning shortly before your coins run out.

A subscription only buys coins. It doesn't unlock any other features. Coins for a month are added when that month's payment goes through; if a payment fails, no coins are added for that month.

New accounts may get a small number of free welcome coins to try things out.

3. Prices, currency and tax

  • Prices are shown in the app before you pay. They can change, but a change never affects what you have already bought.
  • Your currency depends on the country or region on your account: pounds (GBP) in the UK, Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey; euros (EUR) in the euro area; US dollars (USD) everywhere else.
  • Prices in pounds include UK VAT. Prices in euros are the total you pay, including any VAT.
  • Prices in US dollars are the total you pay. If sales tax applies, the app says so (“+ tax”) and it is added at checkout.
  • Your receipt shows the tax included.

4. How you can pay

Payments are handled securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

  • Card, including Apple Pay and Google Pay where your device supports them. Works for coin packs and subscriptions.
  • Bank transfer through Stripe, for coin packs only: in pounds from a UK account, or in euros by SEPA transfer. Choose it on the payment page and pay using the account details and reference shown there (Stripe also emails them). Your coins are added when the money arrives, usually within 1 to 2 working days. Until then, the app shows the order as waiting.

Before you pay, make sure the country on your account is right. The app asks you to confirm it.

5. How long coins last

  • Coins are valid for 6 months from the day they are added to your account. Each lot of coins, whether from a pack, a monthly payment or a gift from us, has its own expiry date.
  • The app shows when your next coins expire, and your receipt shows the expiry date of the coins you bought.
  • About one month before coins expire, we email you a reminder.
  • When you spend coins, the ones that expire soonest are used first.
  • Coins still unused on their expiry date are removed. Expired coins are not refunded.
  • Cancelling a subscription doesn't remove coins you already have. They stay until their own expiry date.

6. Rules for coins

  • Coins are a virtual item for use in Raicita only. They are not money and have no cash value.
  • You can't transfer coins to another account or exchange them back for money.
  • Unused coins are lost if you delete your account.

The full rules are in our Terms of Service.

7. Your right to cancel

Consumers in the UK and the EU normally have 14 days to cancel an online purchase. Because coins are added to your account straight away, the app asks you at checkout to tick a box saying: “I want my coins straight away and understand that I lose my 14-day right of withdrawal once they are added.” You can't buy coins without ticking it. This applies to each payment, including monthly renewals.

8. Refunds

Coins are not refundable, except where the law requires it. For example:

  • your statutory rights as a consumer, for example if something you paid for was faulty;
  • a payment you didn't authorise;
  • coins you paid for that were never added;
  • a valid cancellation within the 14-day period, where the right still applies.

To ask, email support@raicita.app with your payment reference. A person looks at every request. If we refund a payment, the coins it gave you are removed, as far as they haven't been spent.

9. Managing a subscription

  • Go to Me › Manage monthly coins in the app to cancel, see invoices or update your card.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. The month you've already paid for isn't refunded, and you keep your coins until they expire.
  • If a payment fails, the app tells you, and you can update your card to keep getting coins.
  • Deleting your account cancels your subscription.

10. Receipts and records

  • We email you a receipt for every payment. It shows the amount, the tax, the coins you received and when they expire, and our company details.
  • In the app, the Coins screen links to Payments, which lists every payment and its status, and to Coin history, which shows every coin added, spent, returned or expired.
  • Invoices are also available in the Stripe billing portal, which you can open from the app.
