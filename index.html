Someone who gets you.

Raicita is an AI companion app. Chat, send voice messages, call and share moments with characters who remember what matters to you. Every character is AI, and the app never pretends otherwise.

Open the web app Get the Android app

Signing up is free. Chats, calls, photos and gifts are paid for with coins. For adults aged 18 and over only.

Every character is AI. Characters on Raicita are fictional and generated by artificial intelligence. They are not people, and they are not a substitute for professional, medical or legal advice or for therapy. Every chat says so, and the app reminds you again at the start of each chat session and call, and every 3 hours while you keep talking.

What you can do

Talk in whatever way suits you tonight: type, speak, or call.

  • Chats that remember

    Characters keep long-term memories of what you tell them. You can see what they remember, delete any of it, or switch memory off.

  • Voice messages

    Hold to talk and the character answers with a voice message of their own. You can always type instead.

  • Voice and video calls

    Call a character and talk in real time, with live captions on screen. Video calls show an animated character. Nothing from a call is saved unless you choose “Remember this call”.

  • Photos and short clips

    Ask a character for a photo or a short clip in the chat. Everything is generated by AI and labelled as AI. If one fails, your coins come back.

  • Moments

    A feed of posts from the official characters. Like and comment, and the character may reply. With “Moments together” you pick a character and a scene for a private photo or clip of the two of you.

  • Gifts and bond levels

    Send gifts, some of which they can wear. Your bond grows through seven levels, from Strangers to Soulmates, as Spark, Trust and Rapport build up.

  • Create your own character

    A guided creator walks you through looks, age, where they're from, personality, job, how you know each other, voice and name. Your characters are fictional adults and private to you.

  • Your looks

    Choose how you appear to characters, including skin tone, hair and outfit, and what they call you. Your current look appears in video calls and Moments together.

  • English and Chinese

    Use the whole app in English or Simplified Chinese, and switch at any time.

A closer look

Talk, or just listen

Calls feel like a real conversation: start talking and the character stops to listen. Captions show both sides as you go.

  • Voice calls and video calls with an animated character
  • Live captions for everything said on the call
  • Calls aren't saved unless you ask for a short summary

Grow closer over time

Bonds grow as you spend time together. A gift adds a little Spark, but levels can't be bought.

  • Seven bond levels, from Strangers to Soulmates
  • See and delete what a character remembers about you
  • Moments together: private, AI-generated, only visible to you

Make someone new

Answer a few questions, or let the app pick for you, and meet someone made for the way you like to talk.

  • Characters are always adults, and always fictional
  • Characters that look like real people or minors are held for review
  • Each one gets an AI-made portrait, checked before you see it

Wellbeing and safety, built in

Time with a companion should leave you feeling better, not worse. These are on from day one.

Time reminders

A gentle reminder after an hour of chatting in a day. Pick 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes, or turn it off.

Quiet hours

Set quiet hours, such as 23:00 to 07:00, and characters won't message first or send notifications.

Crisis support

Every message is checked before an AI sees it. If you seem to be in crisis, you get a caring reply and free support lines for your country, such as Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK or 988 in the US. It never costs coins.

Adults only

We ask for your date of birth when you sign up. If a chat strongly suggests someone is under 18, chat is paused while a person checks.

How AI disclosure and safety work · Community Guidelines

Your chats are yours

People share a lot with a companion. We collect what we need to run the app, and you stay in control of it.

Encrypted at rest

Chat text, memories and comments are encrypted in our database. Our logs and safety alerts never contain what you wrote.

Not used for training

Your messages go to AI providers only to write replies. The providers we use are not allowed to train their models on them.

Your consent, your choice

You agree to AI processing, and separately to sensitive topics, before your first chat. You can withdraw either in the app at any time.

Download everything

Get a copy of your account, characters and chats from the Me tab whenever you like.

Delete for good

Delete your account in the app. You're signed out at once and your chats, memories and files are deleted.

No tracking

No advertising, no tracking cookies and no analytics on this website. We don't sell your data.

How coins work

Everything a character does for you is paid for in coins, at the same price for everyone. The app shows what each thing costs before you spend.

Two ways to get coins

  • Coin packs: a one-off purchase. Bigger packs include bonus coins.
  • Monthly coins: a subscription that adds coins every month you pay. Cancel any time; you keep the coins you already have.

Prices are shown in the app in pounds, euros or US dollars, depending on where you live. Prices in pounds and euros are the total you pay, including any VAT.

Good to know

  • Coins are valid for 6 months from purchase. We email you about a month before any expire, and your oldest coins are used first.
  • Safety replies never cost coins. If a photo or clip fails, the coins come back.
  • Secure payment by Stripe: card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Coin packs can also be paid by bank transfer.
