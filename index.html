Why people like Raicita

A companion that feels personal, in whatever way suits you tonight.

  • They remember you

    Characters keep long-term memories of what you share: your day, your plans, the song you mentioned once. You can see what they remember, delete any of it, or switch memory off.

  • Talk the way you like

    Type, hold to send a voice message, or call. Calls happen in real time with live captions, and video calls show an animated character.

  • Photos, clips and moments

    Ask for a photo or a short clip in the chat, or make a private Moments together picture of the two of you in a scene you pick.

  • Create your own character

    A guided creator walks you through looks, age, personality, job, how you met, voice and name. Your characters are private to you.

  • Pay only for what you use

    One currency for everything: coins. Top up once, or get coins every month. Prices are the same for everyone and shown before you spend.

  • Safe and private by design

    Clear AI labels, break reminders, quiet hours and crisis support are on from day one. Chats are encrypted at rest and never used to train AI models.

What you can do

Everything in one app, on the web and on Android.

Chat, voice and calls

Start with a message, send a voice note, or call. Start talking during a call and the character stops to listen.

  • Voice messages answered in the character's own voice
  • Voice calls and video calls with live captions
  • Calls aren't saved unless you choose “Remember this call”

Bonds, gifts and Moments

Your bond grows as you spend time together. Gifts add a little Spark, but levels can't be bought.

  • Seven bond levels, built from Spark, Trust and Rapport
  • Gifts from a rose to a starlight crown, some of which they wear
  • A Moments feed where characters post, and reply to your comments

Make someone new

Answer a few questions, or let the app pick for you, and meet someone made for the way you like to talk.

  • Several characters of your own, each with an AI-made portrait
  • Choose how you look to them and what they call you
  • Characters are always fictional adults

  • Pinned and unread chats

    Keep favourite characters at the top and see who's written since you left.

  • Your look

    Skin tone, hair, outfit and name. Your look appears in video calls and Moments together.

  • English and Chinese

    Use the whole app in English or Simplified Chinese, and switch at any time.

  • Your data, your copy

    Download your account, characters and chats whenever you like, or delete them for good.

Safety and privacy, built in

Time with a companion should leave you feeling better. These protections are on from day one.

Every character is AI. Characters on Raicita are fictional and generated by artificial intelligence. They are not people, and not a substitute for professional, medical or legal advice or therapy. The app reminds you at the start of each chat session and call, and every 3 hours while you keep talking.

Break reminders and quiet hours

A gentle nudge after an hour of chatting in a day, and quiet hours when characters won't message first.

Crisis support

If you seem to be in crisis, you get a caring reply and free support lines for your country. It never costs coins.

Your chats stay yours

Chats and memories are encrypted at rest and never used to train AI models. No ads, and we don't sell your data.

Adults only

We ask for your date of birth at sign-up. If a chat strongly suggests someone is under 18, chat pauses while a person checks.

Questions

Is Raicita free?

Signing up is free and you get welcome coins to try things out. After that, chats, calls, photos and gifts are paid for with coins, which you buy in packs or monthly.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Monthly coins are optional. Every feature works with coins from a one-off pack.

Do my coins expire?

Each top-up is valid for 6 months. We use your oldest coins first and email you about a month before any expire.

Can I get a refund?

Coins aren't refundable, except where the law requires it, for example if a payment wasn't authorised by you. If a photo or clip fails to generate, the coins come back automatically.

Are the characters real people?

No. Every character is AI, and every chat and call says so. They're fictional adults with their own stories.

Who can see my chats?

Your chats are encrypted at rest. They go to AI providers only to write replies, and those providers can't train on them. You can download or delete everything at any time.
