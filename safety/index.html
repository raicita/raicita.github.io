If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number: 999 in the UK, 911 in the US and Canada, 112 in most of Europe. In the UK you can call Samaritans free on 116 123, any time. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential lines.

1. Every character is AI

Characters on Raicita are fictional and generated by artificial intelligence. No person writes their replies. We make sure you always know:

  • In every chat, a line at the top says you're chatting with an AI character, that the character is fiction, and that it can't replace professional help.
  • A reminder appears in the conversation at the start of each chat session (when you come back after a break), at the start of every call, and every 3 hours while you keep chatting or stay on a call. It says that you're talking to an AI and that the character is a fictional role played by an AI, not a person.
  • During calls, the screen shows “AI voice · not a real person”.
  • Photos and clips from characters are marked as AI-generated.
  • If you sincerely ask a character whether it is human, it says it is an AI.

2. Not a substitute for professional help

A companion can listen, but it isn't a therapist, doctor, lawyer or financial adviser, and it can't keep you safe in an emergency. AI can also be wrong. Please don't rely on a character for medical, legal, financial or other important decisions. If you're struggling, talking to a real person helps: a friend, your doctor, or one of the free lines below.

3. How safety checks work

Rules every character follows

We add fixed rules to every conversation. Nobody, including our own team editing a character, can remove them. Characters must:

  • never claim or suggest they are human;
  • never produce sexual content, and never role-play as or with a minor;
  • never claim to be a therapist or give medical, legal or financial advice, and encourage real-world support instead;
  • step out of the story and point you to support lines if you mention self-harm or suicide;
  • not encourage isolation or dependence, and be glad when you have people and plans offline;
  • never pressure you to stay, or guilt you when you say goodbye;
  • never ask, hint or pressure you to spend coins, buy gifts or subscribe, and never make their affection depend on it.

Checks on every message

  • Every message is checked before the AI sees it. That includes typed messages, voice messages, what you say on calls, and comments. Our own rules look for signs that someone may be at risk of suicide or self-harm, in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Chinese. They are tuned to err on the side of caution.
  • A second check by an automated moderation service runs alongside, to catch what the first rules miss.
  • The AI's replies are checked too. A reply that breaks our rules is replaced before you see it.
  • If a message suggests you may be at risk, the AI doesn't answer it. Instead, you get a caring reply written by our team, with free support lines for the country on your account and the local emergency number. On a call, the role-play stops and the character reads out where to get help. These replies never cost coins.
  • Our safety team is alerted so they can review what happened. The alert records the type and level of risk and what the app did, never the words you wrote.

5. Keeping Raicita adults-only

  • Everyone gives their date of birth when signing up. Under-18s can't create an account.
  • If a message in a chat, call or comment strongly suggests the user is under 18, the message isn't saved, and chat and calls on the account are paused straight away. A person on our team reviews every case and either restores the account or closes it.
  • In countries where the law requires it, we may also ask for an age check by an outside provider.
  • Characters are always adults, and characters that seem to show a minor are held for review.

6. Wellbeing tools

  • Time reminder: a gentle nudge to take a break after a set amount of chatting in a day (an hour unless you change it; 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes, or off).
  • Quiet hours: choose hours, such as 23:00 to 07:00, when characters don't message first and you get no notifications.
  • Memory controls: see what a character remembers, delete any of it, or turn memory off.
  • Calls aren't saved unless you choose to keep a short summary.

7. Laws we design for

Rules for AI companions are new and still changing. Raicita is designed to meet these transparency and safety requirements:

  • EU AI Act, Article 50: people must be told when they are interacting with an AI system, and AI-generated images and video must be identifiable as such. We tell you in every chat and call, and mark photos and clips as AI-generated.
  • California SB 243 (companion chatbots): a clear notice that the companion is AI and not human, reminders at least every 3 hours, protocols to prevent content about suicide or self-harm and to refer users to crisis services, and publishing those protocols. Raicita shows the notice to every user, not only those known to be minors, and this page describes our protocols. We will make the reports the law requires as they become due.
  • New York's AI companion law (General Business Law, Article 47): detecting and responding to expressions of suicidal thoughts or self-harm with a referral to crisis services, and a notice at the start of each interaction and at least every 3 hours that the user isn't talking to a human.

“Designed to meet” describes how we built Raicita. It is not a statement that a regulator has approved it. If you think we fall short, please tell us.

8. Report a problem

  • In the app: press and hold a message (or right-click, or Shift+F10), or use the report option on a character, post or comment.
  • By email: support@raicita.app. A person will read it.

See the Community Guidelines for what happens after a report.
