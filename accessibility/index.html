Accessibility

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Contents

  1. 1. What works now
  2. 2. Known problems
  3. 3. This website
  4. 4. Tell us what doesn't work for you

We want everyone to be able to use Raicita. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We aren't there yet, and no one outside the team has audited the app so far. This page says honestly where things stand.

1. What works now

  • Buttons and links are real buttons and links, so you can reach them with a keyboard or switch, with a visible focus outline.
  • Icon-only buttons have spoken labels for screen readers, in English and Chinese.
  • Text follows your browser zoom and text size settings.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, we switch animations off.
  • Every voice and video call shows live captions of what you and the character say.
  • Voice messages are optional: you can always type instead.

2. Known problems

  • Photos and clips the characters send have no text description yet.
  • Voice messages from characters don't always show a transcript.
  • Some pop-up panels don't yet keep keyboard focus inside them while open. Press Escape to close one.
  • The message menu opens with a long press or right-click (Shift+F10 on a keyboard); there is no visible button for it yet.
  • The animated character in video calls is decorative and has no audio description; the captions carry the conversation.
  • Some pale secondary text may not meet the recommended colour contrast. We haven't measured every screen yet.
  • We haven't yet tested every screen at high zoom levels or with every screen reader.

We're working through these and will update this page as each one is fixed.

3. This website

This website uses plain HTML with headings, landmarks and a skip link, works with a keyboard, follows your light or dark mode and reduced-motion settings, and gives every image a text description.

4. Tell us what doesn't work for you

If something is hard or impossible to use, or you need information in a different format, email support@raicita.app. Tell us the page and what you were trying to do. A person will reply.

If you're not happy with how we respond, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).
