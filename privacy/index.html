1. The short version

  • Raicita is an AI companion app for adults. To work, it has to process what you say to characters.
  • Your chats are stored encrypted, on servers in the United Kingdom.
  • To write replies, voices and images, parts of your conversation are sent to AI providers, some of them in the United States. Before that, we strip out obvious details like email addresses and phone numbers. Providers are not allowed to use your data to train their models.
  • We ask for your consent before your first chat, and separately for sensitive topics. You can withdraw either at any time.
  • Our logs and safety alerts never contain what you wrote. Our staff can't browse your chats.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account in the app, whenever you like.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking cookies.

5. AI processing

Characters' replies, voices, photos and clips are generated by AI models run by outside providers.

What we send

  • To write a reply: your new message, recent messages in that conversation, relevant memories, the character's description, and what the character calls you and your “who I am” line, if you set them.
  • For voice: your voice message or live call audio, to turn it into text; and the reply text, to turn it into the character's voice.
  • For photos and clips: a description of the character, the scene and, for Moments together, your current look. Your own words are not used as the image prompt.
  • For safety: the text of messages and comments is checked by a moderation service.

What we remove first

Before anything leaves for an AI provider, we automatically replace email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, IBANs and street addresses with placeholders such as “[email]”. Your stored chat is not changed. This catches common formats only. It does not catch names, for example, so please don't share details you'd rather keep to yourself.

What providers may do with it

Providers process your data only to return the result to us. Under our contracts with them, they are not allowed to use it to train their models, and they keep it only as long as they need to provide the service and meet their own legal and abuse-monitoring duties.

6. Who else receives your data

We share personal data only with organisations that help us run Raicita, under contracts that limit what they can do with it, or where the law requires it.

  • AI providers: see section 5.
  • Hosting and storage: our servers, database, file storage and encrypted backups are with cloud providers in the United Kingdom.
  • Payments: Stripe processes card payments, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank transfers, and runs the billing portal. Stripe is also responsible for some data itself, for example for fraud prevention, under its own privacy policy.
  • Email delivery: a provider that sends our emails, such as sign-in links and receipts.
  • Sign-in: Apple or Google, if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Fonts: the web app currently loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it loads them.
  • Age checks: an age assurance provider, only if your country requires one.
  • Advisers and authorities: our professional advisers, and the police, regulators or courts where the law requires it or to protect someone's life or safety.
  • A buyer: if Raicita or our company is sold or reorganised, your data may pass to the new owner under this policy.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

7. International transfers

We store your data in the United Kingdom. Some providers, including most AI providers, process it in the United States or elsewhere. When data leaves the UK or the EU, we rely on one of these safeguards:

  • an adequacy decision or UK adequacy regulations for the destination country;
  • for US providers certified to it, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension;
  • the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses;
  • the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

You can ask us for more information about the safeguard used for a particular provider.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data, and take it with you (portability). In the app, go to Me › Download my data. We prepare a file with your account, characters and chats, and send you a link valid for 7 days.
  • Delete your data. Delete single memories or moments in the app, or your whole account under Me › Delete my account. You're signed out at once, then your data and files are deleted.
  • Correct your data. Edit your profile in the app. To correct your date of birth, contact support.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, under Me (AI processing, Sensitive information) or with the “Remember our conversations” switch. Without the first two, chat and calls are paused.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and restrict processing in some cases.
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects on you (see section 10).

For anything you can't do in the app, email support@raicita.app from the address on your account. We reply within one month, and tell you if we need longer. It's free.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). In the EU, it's the supervisory authority in the country where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to help first.

10. Automated decisions

Some safety decisions are made automatically, because they have to happen instantly:

  • Content moderation: a character may decline to continue, a comment may not be published, or an image may not be delivered.
  • Crisis detection: a message that suggests you may be at risk gets a caring reply and support lines instead of an AI reply. This never costs coins.
  • Possible minor: if a message strongly suggests the user is under 18, or a date of birth given later is under 18, chat and calls on the account are paused at once. A person always reviews this and either restores the account or closes it. While paused, you can still read your chats, change settings, download your data and delete your account.
  • New characters that may show a real person or a minor are held for a person to review.

If you disagree with an automated decision, email support@raicita.app and a person will review it.

11. Children

Raicita is only for adults aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and pause accounts that show strong signs of belonging to someone under 18. If you think a child is using Raicita, please email support@raicita.app. We will close the account and delete the data.

12. How we protect your data

  • Chat text, memories, comments, report notes and your “who I am” line are encrypted in our database (AES-256-GCM), with keys kept outside the database.
  • All connections use TLS encryption.
  • Private files such as your photos and voice messages are only available through links that expire after 15 minutes.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only in hashed form.
  • Staff tools are only reachable over a private network and need two-factor sign-in. Staff get only the access their role needs, and their actions are logged.
  • Backups are encrypted and stored separately from our main servers, in the UK.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

13. Notice for US residents

Some US state privacy laws, such as California's (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA), give residents extra rights. Where these laws apply to us:

  • What we collect, in their categories: identifiers (email, account IDs); commercial information (purchases, coins); internet or other electronic activity (use of the app, device type); audio (voice messages and call audio, which is not recorded); inferences (bond scores with characters); and sensitive personal information (account sign-in, the contents of your messages, and any health, sex life or sexual orientation information you choose to share in chats). Section 3 has the details, and section 6 says who receives it.
  • We use sensitive personal information only to provide the service you ask for and for the other purposes the law allows, such as security and safety. We don't use it to infer characteristics about you.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months.
  • You have the right to know what we collect and how we use it, to access it, to correct it, to delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. Use the in-app tools, or email support@raicita.app. We verify requests through your account email. An authorised agent can ask on your behalf with your signed permission.
  • If we refuse your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If you are still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general.
